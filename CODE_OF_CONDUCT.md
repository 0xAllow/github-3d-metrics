# Code of conduct

Treat participants with respect. Discuss changes and evidence without attacking people. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, and sharing someone else’s private information are unacceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to a repository maintainer using GitHub’s private reporting when available. Maintainers may remove content, limit participation, or block accounts to enforce these expectations. Maintain confidentiality as far as practical and avoid retaliation against anyone who reports a concern.
